Priority with aging

Score = priority level × 15 s + time queued. Low jobs wait longer but can never be starved.

Retries with backoff

Failures retry after an exponential delay with jitter, so a flaky service isn't hammered.

Dead-letter queue

After the last attempt a job is parked with its error, and can be retried by hand.

Rate limits

Token buckets cap emails and data syncs per second. Throttled jobs wait without losing an attempt.

Atomic claims

A Redis Lua script hands each job to exactly one worker, even with many processes.

Crash recovery

Jobs that were running when a process died go back to the queue on restart.
